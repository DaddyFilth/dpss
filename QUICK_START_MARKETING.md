# Quick Start Marketing Guide
## First 7 Days to Launch Your Dropshipping Store

## Day 1: Setup & Foundation ✅

### Technical Setup
- [ ] Update your domain name in all files (search for "your-domain.com")
- [ ] Update social media links in layout.tsx
- [ ] Test all product pages load correctly
- [ ] Set up Google Analytics account
- [ ] Install Facebook Pixel on your site
- [ ] Test checkout process with a real card

### Content Creation
- [ ] Take 10 high-quality product photos
- [ ] Write 5 product descriptions highlighting benefits
- [ ] Create brand logo (use Canva for free)
- [ ] Design email capture popup (use Privy free tier)
- [ ] Set up business Gmail account

## Day 2: Social Media Setup

### Account Creation
- [ ] Create Instagram business account
- [ ] Create TikTok business account  
- [ ] Create Facebook business page
- [ ] Create Pinterest business account
- [ ] Create Twitter/X account

### Profile Setup
- [ ] Upload consistent profile pictures
- [ ] Write compelling bios with call-to-action
- [ ] Add link to your store in all profiles
- [ ] Create highlight covers for Instagram
- [ ] Set up TikTok bio link (use Linktree free)

## Day 3: First Content

### Content Calendar
- [ ] Post 3 product photos to Instagram
- [ ] Create 1 product demonstration video for TikTok
- [ ] Pin 5 products to Pinterest boards
- [ ] Share 1 post to Facebook
- [ ] Tweet about trending products

### Content Types
- Product showcase photos with good lighting
- "How to use" videos for viral products
- Lifestyle content showing products in use
- Before/after photos for beauty products
- Aesthetic room tours for home decor

## Day 4: Launch First Ads

### Facebook/Instagram Ads ($10/day)
- [ ] Set up Meta Business Suite
- [ ] Create product catalog
- [ ] Launch carousel ad with 4 trending products
- [ ] Target interests: home decor, technology, fitness
- [ ] Set up conversion tracking

### Ad Creative
- Use your best product photos
- Write compelling copy with benefits
- Include "Free Shipping" if applicable
- Add social proof (reviews, testimonials)
- Use strong call-to-action

## Day 5: Email Marketing Setup

### Email Service
- [ ] Set up Mailchimp free account
- [ ] Create welcome email sequence (3 emails)
- [ ] Set up abandoned cart automation
- [ ] Create email signup form on your site
- [ ] Add email capture popup (10% discount offer)

### Welcome Sequence
- Email 1: Welcome + 10% discount code
- Email 2: Top trending products
- Email 3: How-to guide + product recommendations

## Day 6: Influencer Outreach

### Find Micro-Influencers
- [ ] Search Instagram for relevant hashtags
- [ ] Find 10-20 influencers with 1K-10K followers
- [ ] Check their engagement rates
- [ ] Review their content quality
- [ ] Build a spreadsheet with contact info

### Outreach Message
```
Hi [Name]! 

I love your content about [their niche]. I recently launched a dropshipping store with viral TikTok products including [mention relevant products].

I'd love to send you some free products to try out in exchange for an honest review on your channel.

Would you be interested? If so, let me know your address and which products catch your eye!

Thanks,
[Your Name]
AI Dropship
```

## Day 7: Analytics & Optimization

### Analytics Setup
- [ ] Set up Google Search Console
- [ ] Submit sitemap.xml to Google
- [ ] Create Google Analytics goals
- [ ] Set up Facebook Pixel events
- [ ] Track key metrics (traffic, conversions, AOV)

### First Week Review
- [ ] Analyze top performing content
- [ ] Review ad performance (CTR, CPC, ROAS)
- [ ] Check email signup rates
- [ ] Monitor social media engagement
- [ ] Plan week 2 based on results

## 🚀 Week 2-4: Scale What Works

### If Facebook Ads Converting (2x+ ROAS)
- Increase daily budget to $20-50
- Scale successful ad sets
- Test new audiences
- Create lookalike audiences
- Add more creatives

### If TikTok Content Performing
- Post daily (1-2 videos per day)
- Engage with comments immediately
- Use trending sounds
- Collaborate with other creators
- Post at optimal times (7-9 AM, 7-9 PM)

### If Email List Growing
- Send weekly newsletter
- Segment by product interests
- Personalize recommendations
- Run flash sales for subscribers
- Encourage referral sharing

### If Products Selling
- Run retargeting ads
- Cross-sell related products
- Ask for reviews and testimonials
- Create bundle deals
- Implement customer loyalty program

## 📊 Key Metrics to Track Daily

### Week 1 Targets
- Traffic: 500-1,000 visitors
- Email Signups: 25-50
- Social Media Followers: 50-100
- Ad Spend: $70 (Facebook $10/day)
- Sales: 2-5 orders
- Revenue: $100-300

### Week 2 Targets
- Traffic: 1,000-2,000 visitors
- Email Signups: 50-100
- Social Media Followers: 100-250
- Ad Spend: $140
- Sales: 5-10 orders
- Revenue: $300-700

### Week 4 Targets
- Traffic: 3,000-5,000 visitors
- Email Signups: 200-500
- Social Media Followers: 500-1,000
- Ad Spend: $280
- Sales: 15-25 orders
- Revenue: $1,000-2,000

## 🎯 Pro Tips for Maximum Impact

### Viral Product Marketing
- Focus heavily on sunset lamp, mushroom light, ice roller
- Use "TikTok made me buy it" angle
- Create aesthetic content for Instagram
- Jump on trending sounds and hashtags

### Smart Home Products
- Target tech enthusiasts
- Create comparison videos
- Highlight AI features
- Focus on future-forward messaging
- Use tech review communities

### Beauty Products
- Partner with beauty micro-influencers
- Create transformation content
- Use #morningskincare hashtag
- Focus on before/after results
- Leverage user testimonials

### Pricing Strategy
- Use psychological pricing ($19.99 vs $20)
- Create bundle deals
- Offer free shipping threshold
- Use urgency tactics (limited time offers)
- Implement email-only discounts

### Content Frequency
- Instagram: 3-5 posts/week
- TikTok: 1-2 videos/day
- Pinterest: 5-10 pins/day
- Email: 1-2 emails/week
- Facebook: 2-3 posts/week

## 🆘 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Don't:
- Spend too much on ads before validating products
- Ignore analytics and data
- Post inconsistent content
- Forget to engage with your audience
- Neglect email marketing
- Copy competitors exactly
- Overwhelm customers with too many choices

### Do:
- Start small and scale what works
- Use data to make decisions
- Be authentic and genuine
- Engage with comments and messages
- Build your email list continuously
- Find your unique angle
- Curate products for your audience
